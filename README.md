# Eventbrite vs Meetup vs AllEvents: Which Is Best for Listing Events in 2026?

Eventbrite, Meetup and AllEvents are three of the most popular places to list events online, but they work in very different ways. Eventbrite is a large ticketing platform. In 2025, it issued **258 million tickets for 4.6 million events**, according to [Eventbrite's Q4 2025 earnings summary](https://quartr.com/events/eventbrite-inc-eb-q4-2025_3Y1Yb8XZ). Meetup is built around recurring community groups, and its organizer subscription covers up to **three groups with unlimited members**, according to [Meetup's Help Center](https://help.meetup.com/hc/en-us/articles/360001620472). AllEvents is an event discovery platform that says it helps **15 million people** find events every month.

All three have changed recently. Bending Spoons completed its purchase of Eventbrite in March 2026, Meetup raised organizer prices after a 2024 ownership change, and AllEvents has grown fast as a free discovery platform, especially in India.

In this guide, I'll compare Eventbrite vs Meetup vs AllEvents on fees, features, audience and ease of use, explain the AllEvents.in vs Eventbrite difference, and help you pick the best platform for your event.

## What Is the Quick Answer?

**Choose Eventbrite for ticketed events, Meetup for recurring community groups and AllEvents for free event discovery and local reach.**

- **Eventbrite:** Best for selling tickets to conferences, workshops, concerts and paid events
- **Meetup:** Best for building a recurring community, like a tech group, hiking club or book club
- **AllEvents:** Best for free listings and getting discovered by people searching for events in their city

Many organizers use more than one. You can sell tickets on Eventbrite, build a community on Meetup and list the same event on AllEvents for extra reach.

## What Is Eventbrite?

**Eventbrite is an event ticketing and registration platform used to create event pages, sell tickets, manage attendees and check people in.**

**Best for:** Paid events, workshops, conferences, festivals, concerts and webinars

**Key features:**
- Free and paid ticketing
- Event pages and registration forms
- Built-in event discovery
- Organizer app for check-in
- Email marketing tools (more with paid plans)
- Online and in-person events

**Fees (US):** Free events cost nothing. Paid tickets are reported at about **3.7% + USD 1.79 per ticket**, plus **2.9% payment processing** per order.

**Pros:** Trusted brand, large audience, strong ticketing tools
**Cons:** Fees on paid tickets, limited design options, lots of competing events

## What Is Meetup?

**Meetup is a community platform where organizers create groups around shared interests and host regular events for members.**

**Best for:** Recurring community events, networking groups, hobby clubs and local meetups

**Key features:**
- Interest-based groups
- RSVP management
- Member messaging
- Group discovery by location and topic
- Recurring events

**Fees:** Organizers pay a subscription to run groups. Third-party reviews put the Standard plan at about **USD 29.99 per month** or **USD 174.99 per year**, though Meetup says prices vary by location and app store. Meetup Pro, for brands with many groups, costs more.

**Pros:** Strong community focus, built-in local discovery, great for repeat attendance
**Cons:** Organizer subscription costs, less flexible ticketing, not ideal for one-off events

## What Is AllEvents?

**AllEvents (AllEvents.in) is an event discovery platform that helps people find events in their city and lets organizers list and promote events, often for free.**

**Best for:** Free event listings, local discovery, concerts, workshops, festivals and city events, especially in India and other fast-growing markets

**Key features:**
- Free event listings
- City-based event discovery
- Ticketing with low fees
- Event promotion tools
- Website and social media widgets

**Fees:** Listing is free. AllEvents says its ticketing fee is about **USD 1 per ticket**, charged to attendees, though fees can vary by country and plan.

**Pros:** Free listing, strong local discovery, simple setup
**Cons:** Smaller brand in the US than Eventbrite, fewer community features than Meetup

## AllEvents.in vs Eventbrite: What's the Difference?

**AllEvents.in is mainly an event discovery and listing platform, while Eventbrite is mainly a ticketing and event management platform.**

- **Main purpose:** AllEvents focuses on discovery. Eventbrite focuses on ticketing and registration.
- **Listing cost:** Both let you list free events at no cost.
- **Ticket fees:** AllEvents says it charges a small flat fee, while Eventbrite charges a percentage plus a fixed fee.
- **Audience:** Eventbrite has a large global audience. AllEvents is strong in city-based discovery, especially in India.
- **Best use:** Use Eventbrite for ticket sales and AllEvents for extra reach.

## Eventbrite vs Meetup: What's the Difference?

**Eventbrite is best for one-off and ticketed events, while Meetup is best for ongoing groups and recurring community events.**

- **Event type:** Eventbrite suits single events. Meetup suits regular gatherings.
- **Cost model:** Eventbrite charges per ticket. Meetup charges organizers a subscription.
- **Community:** Meetup builds a member base. Eventbrite focuses on attendees per event.
- **Ticketing:** Eventbrite has stronger ticketing tools.

## Meetup vs AllEvents: What's the Difference?

**Meetup is built for groups and memberships, while AllEvents is built for listing and discovering individual events.**

- **Cost:** Meetup requires a paid organizer plan. AllEvents listings are free.
- **Community:** Meetup has stronger group and member features.
- **Discovery:** AllEvents shows events by city and category.

## Which Platform Is Best for Your Event?

**Pick the platform that matches your event type, budget and audience.**

- **Paid conference or workshop:** Eventbrite
- **Monthly networking group:** Meetup
- **Free local event:** AllEvents
- **Webinar or online event:** Eventbrite or AllEvents
- **Community club or hobby group:** Meetup
- **Events in India:** AllEvents, plus Eventbrite or local ticketing sites
- **Small budget:** AllEvents or Eventbrite for free events

**Yes. Many organizers sell tickets on one platform and list the event on others to reach more people.**

1. **Create your main event page** on Eventbrite or your own website
2. **List it on AllEvents** with a link to buy tickets
3. **Post it in your Meetup group** if you have one
4. **Share it on social media** and email
5. **List it on other free event sites** and local calendars

## What Mistakes Should You Avoid When Listing Events?

**Avoid incomplete event details, poor images, inconsistent information across sites and ignoring platform fees.**

- **Missing key details**, like time zone or location
- **Using low-quality images**
- **Different dates or prices** on different platforms
- **Ignoring ticket fees** when setting prices
- **Not updating listings** after changes
- **Listing too late** to build interest

## Final Thoughts

Eventbrite vs Meetup vs AllEvents comes down to what kind of event you're running. Eventbrite is best for selling tickets and managing registrations, Meetup is best for building recurring communities and AllEvents is best for free listings and local discovery. In the AllEvents.in vs Eventbrite comparison, AllEvents wins on free reach and low fees, while Eventbrite wins on ticketing tools and global brand trust.

For the best results, don't rely on just one platform. Use the one that fits your main goal, then list your event on others to reach more people.
